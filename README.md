## 👋 About Me

I'm a full stack engineer who enjoys building complete products end to end — from responsive, well-structured frontends in Vue and React to asynchronous backend systems that stay reliable under real-world load. My work sits at the intersection of solid full stack engineering and applied AI, where I build both the interfaces people use and the infrastructure that lets AI features actually work in production, not just in a notebook.

🔧 On the **backend**, I design clean, modular architectures for complex operational problems — logistics, automation, real-time tracking — using **FastAPI**, **Flask**, **PostgreSQL**, and background job processing with **Celery** and **Redis**.

🗄️ I spend a lot of time in the **database layer**: writing complex SQL queries, designing normalized relational schemas, and working with stored procedures, triggers, and indexing across **PostgreSQL**, **MySQL**, and **SQLite**.

🎨 On the **frontend**, I focus on intuitive, role-aware interfaces with **Vue 3**, **Pinia**, and **Tailwind CSS** that make complex systems feel simple to use.

☁️ Beyond the core stack, I wire cloud and AI services into products — **Azure Cognitive Services**, **Gemini/OpenAI APIs**, **Streamlit** for quick data apps, and automation scripts for audio/video processing.

I'm always looking for the next interesting full stack, database, or AI systems problem to dig into.
